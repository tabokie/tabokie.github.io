title: Sorry, I quit early

A month ago, I made the decision to stop being a professional programmer.

This career was a short one, a little over 6 years, but it treated me well. Knowing how to program and loving it is a blessing. You would have courage to build whatever the mind can hold, and see it get built in the speed of your thoughts (or fingers). In between, you would take the pleasure to express an idea unambiguously and work it so close to perfection. Fortunate I am, to have built much and built them with great mentors. Their work ethic and artistic spirit have guided me and will continue to do so.

However, to program in a professional setting is no longer rewarding due to the way people and resources are organized. This industry is morphing into the same shape as the gigantic borderless platforms it has built. Everything appears reachable at the fingertips yet we are never so detached from the value we create. We don't see it, we don't own it, and increasingly it vaporizes into thin air if ever existed. Coding agents made it worse by demeaning the day-to-day programming from a practice of the heart and a labor with both hands to an endless race for more work - to build because you can, not because you should. Feels like a good time to move on.

Leaving made me empty for a while. It feels like taking off the prescription lens and not knowing where to resume focus. In the liminal space of blindness and fear, I realized I am not the type of person who can enjoy exploring the world without a purpose in mind. I must treat life as a coherent body of work, such that each moment passed is not mere sand slipping through fingers, it would amount to something, something I take responsibility of shaping. Being able to think of life that way comforts me deeply. Whether I have the ability to shape it the way I want is unimportant. I accept I will fail at it. Countless people have failed while trying, it should be considered a given fact by now. A wiser question is, will the path and its responsibility uplift me towards a state of mind, where I can put my potential to better use? Answer it, and you shall have young and fearless dreams like before.
